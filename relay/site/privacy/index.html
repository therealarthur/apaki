Apaki

Privacy

Last changed 12 October 2026

Myrlin Apaki is a desktop app for Claude Code, with phone apps and a web page that reach your PC through a relay at apaki.myrlin.io. Myrlin Labs (Arthur Mouradian) makes it. This page says what each part collects, what it keeps, and who else is involved. The source code is public, so you can check every statement here against it.

The desktop app

Everything Apaki works with stays on your PC: your Claude Code and Codex sessions, your logins, the search index and the local model behind search by meaning, your settings and your workspace. Apaki sends none of it to us. Claude Code talks to Anthropic, and Codex to OpenAI, exactly as they do without Apaki; their own privacy policies cover that. Apaki has no analytics and no crash reporting.

Until you turn on phone access, Apaki makes no connection to our relay or our site.

Phone access and the relay

With phone access on, your PC and your paired phones connect to the relay, and everything between them is encrypted end to end (a P-256 key exchange, AES-256-GCM, fresh keys for every connection). The relay cannot read your prompts, replies, files or session list, and it cannot pair a phone or connect to your PC.

What the relay does handle:

  • IP addresses, when your PC and phones connect and leave, and how much data moves.
  • To keep the relay's cost capped and shared fairly, a count of each day's use per address (an IP address, or the /64 network of an IPv6 address). The count holds no content and is deleted when the day ends (UTC).

The relay's code writes no logs, and the hosting platform's request logging is turned off in its configuration. The relay runs on Cloudflare, which sees every request as the network and TLS provider; Cloudflare's privacy policy covers what it does. If you would rather not use our relay at all, you can run your own.

Apaki accounts (optional)

An Apaki account is a passkey, with no email address and no password. For an account, the relay keeps the name you give it, your passkeys' public keys, the devices you link, and one vault listing your PCs, encrypted with a key only your passkey can produce, so we cannot open it. Deleting the account from any of your phones deletes everything the server holds for it.

The iPhone app

The app uses the camera only to scan the pairing code on your PC's screen; no picture is kept or sent. It keeps its device key in the Secure Enclave and its pairings on the phone. It has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. If you join the TestFlight beta, Apple shares with us what you choose to send through TestFlight (feedback, screenshots, crash reports), under Apple's own terms.

This website

apaki.myrlin.io sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and loads nothing from other sites. The web version of the phone app stores its pairings and device key in your browser so it can reconnect.

Sharing, selling, children

We do not sell or rent data, and we share none beyond the providers named above. Apaki is not directed at children under 13.
