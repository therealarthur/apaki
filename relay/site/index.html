Myrlin Apaki

This address connects a phone to Myrlin Apaki on a computer. Open the pairing code in Apaki on your PC and scan it; the code opens the exact phone page that matches your Apaki.

What this site can and cannot see

  • It serves the phone page and relays frames between your PC and your phones. Everything in those frames is end-to-end encrypted between the two; this site holds no keys and stores nothing.
  • The one-time pairing secret is in the part of the link after #, which browsers never send to a server.
  • Your Claude and Codex logins never leave your PC. A phone can only use its own conversations and the session list.
  • Every version of the phone page has its own address and never changes. Before your PC shows a pairing code, it builds the page it expects from its own code and checks, file by file, that this site serves exactly that.
