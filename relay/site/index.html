Myrlin Apaki

Your PC's Claude Code sessions, on your phone or any browser, end to end encrypted.

Open Apaki

Sign in with your Apaki account (a passkey) to see your PCs and connect, or pair a new phone with the code Apaki shows on your PC (Phone, at the bottom of its sidebar).

What this site can and cannot see

  • It serves the phone page and relays frames between your PC and your phones. Everything in those frames is end-to-end encrypted between the two; this site cannot read them and keeps nothing about your conversations. If you make an Apaki account, it stores the account's name, its passkeys' public keys and a vault it cannot open.
  • The one-time pairing secret is in the part of the link after #, which browsers never send to a server.
  • Your Claude and Codex logins never leave your PC. A paired phone works in conversations on your PC as you would there, so Claude can run commands and edit files for it; billing, accounts and pairing stay on the PC.
  • Every version of the phone page has its own address and never changes. Before your PC shows a pairing code, it builds the page it expects from its own code and checks, file by file, that this site serves exactly that.
